Quick Status Guide

  • CHECKED_IN — Tagged at origin airport check-in desk or port intake.
  • LOADED — Sorted and loaded into aircraft belly container or vessel hold.
  • IN_TRANSIT — Actively in movement between departure and destination.
  • MISPLACED — Auto-flagged if IN_TRANSIT with no checkpoint scan for > 4 hours.
  • CLAIMED — Successfully received by passenger or port consignee.
